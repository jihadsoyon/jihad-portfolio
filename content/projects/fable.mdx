## Overview

Fable is an ebook marketplace with three roles: readers, writers and admins.

## Key Features

- Readers can discover and purchase ebooks
- Writers publish and manage their work after verification
- Admins oversee the platform through role-based dashboards

## Tech & Decisions

Built with React and Tailwind CSS, styled with DaisyUI. Role-based access keeps each dashboard separate and secure.
