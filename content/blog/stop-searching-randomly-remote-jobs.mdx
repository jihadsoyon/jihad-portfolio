Getting a remote job or your first international freelance client can feel difficult, especially when you don't know where to look.

The good news is that you don't need to search everywhere. There are several platforms specifically designed to connect developers, designers, marketers, writers, and other professionals with companies and clients around the world.

Here are 15 platforms worth checking if you're serious about building a freelance or remote career.

## 💼 Freelance Marketplaces

### 1. [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/)

Fiverr is great for selling specific services as packages, known as gigs. You can offer services such as web development, UI design, AI integration, WordPress, writing, and more. Fiverr currently covers hundreds of service categories.

**Best for:** Beginners building their first freelance profile.

### 2. [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/)

Upwork works more like a traditional freelance marketplace. Clients post projects, and freelancers submit proposals. It has dedicated categories for Development & IT, Design, Writing, Marketing, and many others.

**Best for:** Long-term freelance projects and recurring clients.

### 3. [PeoplePerHour](https://www.peopleperhour.com/)

PeoplePerHour connects businesses with freelancers through fixed-price offers, project proposals, and direct freelancer hiring.

**Best for:** Web development, design, writing, and business services.

### 4. [Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.com/)

Freelancer gives you access to projects from clients around the world. You can search by skill, participate in contests, and bid on projects.

**Best for:** Beginners looking for a large variety of projects.

### 5. [Arc](https://arc.dev/)

Arc focuses heavily on remote developer opportunities and connects software professionals with companies looking for technical talent.

**Best for:** Developers targeting remote tech jobs.

### 6. [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/)

Toptal positions itself as an exclusive network representing the top 3% of freelance talent across software development, design, management consulting, product, and related fields.

**Best for:** Experienced professionals seeking premium clients.

### 7. [Gun.io](https://gun.io/)

Gun.io is focused strongly on connecting experienced software developers with companies that need engineering talent.

**Best for:** Professional software developers.

### 8. [Turing](https://www.turing.com/)

Turing connects technical professionals with remote opportunities and focuses on software developers, data professionals, and other technical roles.
